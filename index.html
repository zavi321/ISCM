꧁༺Introduction༻꧂

Where Faith Meets Modern Learning

Islamia School & College Martung is committed to providing students with a balanced combination of modern education, Islamic values, moral training, and character development — nurturing a generation that excels academically while remaining firmly connected to Islamic teachings.

The school's vision extends beyond the classroom, helping students build strong communication skills, critical thinking, leadership, confidence, and a sense of responsibility toward society, family, and community.

꧁༺Our Journey༻꧂

Our History

Islamia School & College Martung (The ISCM) was established in 2017 with the great vision of providing the children of the region with a quality educational environment — one where Islamic education, high moral character, and modern knowledge come together in perfect harmony.

The institution was founded by sincere, capable, and education-loving individuals: Sir Ibad Ur Rahman and Sir Hilal Ahmad. Their goal was not merely to establish a school, but to raise a generation adorned with knowledge, character, faith, and morality — one capable of serving their society and country in the best possible way.

The institution began its educational journey with the Nursery class. By the grace of Allah Almighty, the visionary leadership of its founders, the tireless efforts of its teachers, and the full trust of parents, the institution achieved new milestones of progress every year. New classes were added step by step, until today, Alhamdulillah, Islamia School & College Martung (The ISCM) has successfully reached up to Intermediate (FSc/FA) level.

Our effort is to provide students with an education that makes them successful in academic, moral, and practical fields. In sha Allah, the institution will continue its educational journey toward higher classes in the future, while further raising its academic standards.

"Knowledge, character, and faith — this is our identity, our asset, and our commitment."
